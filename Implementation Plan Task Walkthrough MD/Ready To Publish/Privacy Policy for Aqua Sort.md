## Introduction

WebSpider Studios ("we", "our", "us") developed Aqua Sort, a mobile water-sorting puzzle game. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Aqua Sort app ("App") on Android devices. By downloading or using the App, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Account Registration Data (Optional)
If you choose to create an account, we collect:
- **Email address** — used for authentication, account recovery, and security notifications.
- **Display name** — publicly shown on leaderboards and in multiplayer sessions.
- **Phone number** — optional; used only for two-factor security verification.
- **Profile avatar** — an optional image you may upload.

Guest users may play without registration. Guest progress is stored locally only and may be lost if the app is uninstalled.

### b) Game Progress Data
- Coin balance, owned cosmetic skins, unlocked levels, and game history.
- If you are registered, this data is synced to our Supabase database servers. If you play as a guest, this data is stored locally on your device only.

### c) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
The free version of Aqua Sort displays ads powered by **Google AdMob**. When viewing advertisements, Google's AdMob SDK may automatically collect:
- Android Advertising ID (AAID)
- IP address and general geographic location
- Device identifiers and usage signals
- App interaction data for ad relevance

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time by going to: **Device Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation**.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### d) In-App Purchase Data
Aqua Sort offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and a premium subscription) processed exclusively through **Google Play Billing**. We do **not** collect or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation of successful purchase to unlock the corresponding in-app content.

### e) Multiplayer & Leaderboard Data
- Your **display name** and **score** are stored on our servers and may be visible to other players on public leaderboards and in multiplayer sessions.
- We do not display your email address, phone number, or any other private information publicly.

### f) Authentication via Third-Party OAuth
If you choose to sign in via **Google** or **Facebook**, those providers may share your name and email address with us according to their own privacy policies. We use this only to create and authenticate your Aqua Sort account.

### g) Technical / Diagnostic Data
Anonymous crash reports and error logs (containing no personal data) may be collected to help us identify and fix bugs and improve app stability.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All data transmitted between the App and our servers is protected using **TLS/SSL encryption**.
- Data is stored on **Supabase** servers, which use enterprise-grade encryption at rest and in transit.
- We implement **Row Level Security (RLS)** in our database, ensuring each user can only access their own data.
- Account-sensitive changes (such as email address changes) require dual-email verification through our Zero Casualization security protocol.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- If you delete your account, your profile data will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.
- Local data (SharedPreferences) stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### GDPR (European Union / UK) Users
- **Right of Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Request deletion of your account and all personal data.
- **Right to Data Portability**: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object**: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.

### CCPA (California) Users
- **Right to Know**: Request disclosure of what personal information we collect.
- **Right to Delete**: Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Right to Opt-Out**: Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data).
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **webspiderstudios@gmail.com**

You may also delete your account directly from within the App:  
**Profile → Reset Progress & Data → Delete Account**
