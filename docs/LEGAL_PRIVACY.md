# Privacy Policy for Aqua Sort

**Last Updated: April 14, 2026**

WebSpider Studios ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our game, Aqua Sort (the "Game").

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Core Identity**: We collect your Email Address and Phone Number (optional) for account authentication and recovery.
- **Game Status**: We sync your coin balance, owned skins, and leaderboard scores to our secure cloud servers (Supabase).
- **Security Logs**: For the "Purity" high-security system, we log verification attempts and security challenges.

## 2. Purity Security Protocol
To protect your account, any changes to your core identity (Email/Phone) are gated by our "Zero Casualization" security protocol, requiring multi-step verification.

## 3. Data Storage
Our cloud data is stored using Supabase, which employs enterprise-grade encryption and Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure your data is only accessible by you.
